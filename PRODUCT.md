# void-terminal

An unofficial bilingual Genshin Impact source archive and research terminal. Readers browse original records and ask questions grounded in immutable knowledge snapshots; citations lead back to their source documents. It is a Next.js website backed by the existing Python knowledge service and SQLite/PostgreSQL storage.

The same open-source codebase and Web image support two runtime deployment modes. `DEPLOYMENT_MODE=self-hosted` is the default: readers supply their own model API key, with no account, email, credits, device checks, contact or sponsorship UI. Requests travel from the browser through the reader's deployed instance to the selected model provider. Keys remain in page memory and clear on reload; the instance operator can access keys in transit. Provider URL and model preferences may persist locally, but keys do not.

`DEPLOYMENT_MODE=hosted` is the official site's account-only experience: email-code login, a first-party device check and free credits, with an operator-configured model. It has no BYOK settings, custom provider controls or mode switch, and unavailable authentication, model configuration or budget never falls back to BYOK. Invalid deployment values fail closed. Credits use an independent SQLite ledger with either corpus backend. Append-only credit grants, an internal order/payment core and separate ledger recovery tools are reserved for future use. Credit purchases, checkout and subscriptions are disabled. Self-hosted guest history and hosted account history remain separate local namespaces; sign-in does not sync chats across devices, and an identity outage must not expose guest or another account's history.

Eligible hosted accounts get three daily credits at Shanghai midnight and a one-time 20-credit welcome gift by default. Standard research costs one credit and deep research costs three. Daily credits are used first; unused daily credits do not accumulate, while reserve credits remain until spent. The account panel separates daily credits, reserve, temporary holds and the collapsed record of grants and usage. Device verification, insufficient credits and shared budget denials preserve the unsent question and the current branch. Readers who can afford standard research may switch from deep research and decide when to send again.

Sponsorship channels and the contact email appear only when configured on the hosted site. Sponsorship is voluntary and never issues credits. Optional routes include verified QR images, creator page links and a public BNB Smart Chain mainnet address with explicit accepted assets. The site does not connect wallets or initiate transfers. Readers may request a manual grant using a public contact email; they review and send the message themselves. The template carries the account ID, purpose and requested amount without chat content or device data.

The source-reading module is 尘世文卷 / Teyvat Archive. The three archive navigation entries use original book, bag and character-list UI icons. Character filters, cards and identity use the original seven elemental symbols with localized accessible labels. Below the avatar strip, character identity reads element icon, bold name, then rarity; the second line joins title and weapon type with a middle dot. The detail element has no visible text label. On wide screens, the base-stat heading shares the name's top alignment, with the artwork beneath identity; narrow screens stack identity, artwork and stats. Asset provenance is recorded alongside the local sprites; unknown elements are never guessed.

The Compendium (万象集录) serves readers looking for weapon statistics, artifact stat rules, descriptions and stories of inventory items. It presents records from the source datasets, not a player's owned inventory or a claim that every record is currently obtainable. Internal records are hidden by default. Missing upstream information remains visibly missing.

The approved museum experience is a game inventory: rarity-colored image grid and independently scrolling detail panel, with a full-screen detail dialog on phones. The existing Sumeru scenery, paper/slate/gold palette, sidebar, bilingual preferences, dark theme, history, source reader and settings remain the product's shared shell. The title uses a licensed self-hosted Noto Serif subset; body typography remains unchanged.

Item images must be source-verified assets. Item data, linked documents and item questions stay bound to one immutable snapshot. Asking about an item prepares a new question without sending it and retains the previous draft for restoration. The museum has no account inventory, gacha, build simulator, damage calculation or artifact scoring.

The Character Archive (群星图鉴) at `/characters` is a dedicated game-style character menu: searchable portrait cards, an avatar strip, original character artwork, and Attributes, Constellations, Talents, Profile and Cultivation sections. Attributes use legal source level/ascension pairs without equipment buffs. Traveler forms are separated by exact skill-depot identity. Stories retain alternate source branches and unlock conditions. Four voice languages are independently selectable and original audio loads only on explicit playback; changing character, entry, language or section stops the previous player.

Character data, material links and source reading share one immutable snapshot. Source assets are verified before display, and audio archive membership is distinguished from individually decoded playback. Missing portraits, audio, numbers or unresolved text are reported rather than invented. Formal source flags do not prove release or obtainability. The archive is a reference tool, with no player account state, simulated constellation activation, fabricated star geometry or generated voice acting. The existing bilingual shell, themes, question drafts and mobile navigation remain shared.
